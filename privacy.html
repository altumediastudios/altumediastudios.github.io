Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy describes how Altumedia Social Engine ("the App"), operated by Altumedia Studios, handles data. The App is an internal tool used only by Altumedia Studios to publish its own video content to its own social media accounts.

Data we access

  • Google / YouTube: OAuth tokens and basic channel information (channel ID and name) for the channels we own, used solely to upload and schedule our videos via the YouTube Data API.
  • Instagram / Facebook (via Facebook Login): Page and Instagram professional account IDs, username and Page access token for our own accounts, used solely to publish our Reels and Stories.
  • TikTok: open ID, display name and access token for our own accounts, used solely to publish our videos via the Content Posting API.

How data is stored

Tokens are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) on a private computer controlled by Altumedia Studios. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or profiling.

Third-party users

The App does not collect personal data from viewers or from any third-party users.

Google API Services

The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API Services are used in accordance with the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and data deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions, Facebook settings (Business integrations), Instagram settings (Apps and websites) or TikTok settings (Manage app permissions). To request deletion of stored data, email infoaltumedia@gmail.com; data is deleted within 30 days.
